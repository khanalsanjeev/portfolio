Accounting & FinanceProfessional work

Financial Reporting & Accounting

Preparation and organization of financial information, reporting, accounting records and compliance-related work for organizations.

Scope of work

Financial reporting: preparation and review of financial statements and supporting schedules, with attention to consistency, completeness and applicable reporting requirements.

Accounting: organizing transaction records, reconciliations, ledger reviews and supporting documentation to improve the reliability of financial information.

Records and compliance: helping businesses structure incomplete or fragmented records and maintain information needed for statutory and management reporting.

Financial AdvisoryProfessional work

Financial Modelling & Credit Analysis

Building financial models and evaluating business funding requirements to support financing discussions and informed decisions.

Scope of work

Model development: translating business assumptions into integrated projections, including revenue, operating costs, working capital, cash flow and financing requirements.

Credit analysis: examining historical performance, repayment capacity, projected cash flows and key financial indicators relevant to bank financing.

Decision support: preparing structured analysis and supporting schedules that help explain the business case, funding need and financial feasibility.

Business FinanceProfessional work

Business Analysis & Advisory Support

Using financial and operational information to understand business conditions, identify issues and develop practical analysis.

Scope of work

Business understanding: reviewing business activities, operating context and financial information to understand how an organization generates and uses resources.

Problem analysis: identifying financial and process-related questions that need attention, then organizing available information into a usable picture.

Practical outputs: developing analysis, reports, models and presentation materials to support management discussions and business decisions.

Technology & SystemsIndependent initiative

1Pana — Managed Accounting Solution

A managed accounting solution concept for SMEs, combining accounting operations with a structured software and reporting system.

Project details

Why it was developed: to explore a more organized and technology-enabled way for small and medium-sized businesses to manage accounting, reporting and recurring tax workflows.

What was built: a working product concept supported by database architecture, automated double-entry logic, reporting structures and tax-related workflows.

Development: approximately six months of effort, comprising three months of core development followed by three months of operational refinement and testing.

Stage: developed as an MVP and refined through practical testing. The project demonstrates applied work across accounting, product thinking and systems design.

VenturesIndependent initiative

Nepalventure — Startup & Capital Initiative

An initiative exploring how early-stage and growing businesses could connect with potential sources of investment.

Project details

Why it was developed: to address the gap between promising business ideas and access to capital, and to create a structured point of connection between emerging ventures and potential investors.

What was explored: the platform concept, startup and investor journeys, venture information requirements and possible models for connecting businesses with eligible investors.

Key consideration: the model was reconsidered in light of Nepal's securities and investment regulatory environment, with attention to a more appropriate and compliant structure.

Stage: concept and model exploration. It represents an early practical expression of an ongoing interest in venture development and capital allocation, rather than an operating investment platform.
